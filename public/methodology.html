1 Mathematical Formulas Used Across All Modules

GHG Protocol Scope 1, 2 & 3 Tier-1/2 Methods

Below are the exact mathematical equations implemented in lib/carbon.ts, lib/data/climate-knowledge.ts, lib/api/simulations.ts, and lib/store.ts.

MOD-01 Daily Activity Multi-Modal Tracking Track Screen

Computes gross emissions across 5 categories: Travel, Food, Electricity, Shopping, and Waste.

DAILY LOG EQUATION
E_item = Quantity × Factor_category

E_daily_total = ∑ E_travel + ∑ E_food + ∑ E_elec + ∑ E_shop + ∑ E_waste
  • Travel Factors: Petrol Car (0.18 kg/km), Bus (0.08), Metro (0.04), Flight (0.25), Bike (0)
  • Food Factors: Beef meal (5.0 kg/item), Chicken (1.2), Veg (0.6), Vegan (0.4)
  • Electricity Factor: Grid average (0.45 kg CO₂e / kWh)
  • Shopping: New clothing (10 kg), Electronics (30 kg), Online delivery (2.5 kg)
  • Waste: General bag (0.5 kg), Food waste (0.4 kg), Plastic bottle (0.2 kg)
Worked Example: 10 km car commute + 1 beef lunch + 4 kWh AC:
(10 × 0.18) + (1 × 5.0) + (4 × 0.45) = 1.8 + 5.0 + 1.8 = 8.6 kg CO₂e/day.
MOD-02 Action Plan Habit Commitments Actions Screen

Calculates verified weekly carbon abatement when user activates micro-commitments.

HABIT ABATEMENT EQUATION
ΔE_action = Baseline_emissions_week - Shifted_emissions_week

E_weekly_score = 100 - [ (E_week - Goal_week) / Peer_average ] × 100
  • Metro Swap (2 days): Saves 8.0 kg CO₂e/week (Easy)
  • Two Meat-Free Days: Saves 6.4 kg CO₂e/week (Easy)
  • AC 30-min Timer: Saves 2.1 kg CO₂e/week (Easy)
  • Carpool Long Trips: Saves 5.5 kg CO₂e/week (Medium)
  • Buy 1 Fewer Item (2nd-hand): Saves 9.0 kg CO₂e/week (Hard)
Worked Example: Activating Metro + Meat-Free + AC Timer reduces weekly footprint by 8.0 + 6.4 + 2.1 = 16.5 kg CO₂e/week (~858 kg/year).

03 Carbon Twin Engine Simulate Screen

Runs counterfactual behavioral forecasting before lifestyle decisions are committed.

COUNTERFACTUAL FORECASTING MATRIX
ΔE_weekly = Frequency_wk × Distance × (EF_baseline - EF_alternative)

ΔE_annual = ΔE_weekly × 52 weeks

Trees_equivalent = ΔE_annual ÷ 21.77 kg/tree/year
  • Modal Shift Reduction: (0.171 - 0.035) / 0.171 = 79.5% direct cut
  • Gasoline Avoided: Gallons = ΔE_annual ÷ 8.887 kg/gal
  • Credit Minting: Credits = ⌊ ΔE_weekly × 10 ⌋
Worked Example: Swapping 3 car trips (15km) to metro weekly eliminates 3 × 15 × (0.171 - 0.035) = 6.12 kg/week (318 kg/yr = 14.6 Tree-years + 61 Eco Credits).

UTILITY GRID EMISSION AUDIT
E_electricity_bill = Units_kWh_extracted × EF_subregion_grid

5 End-to-End Enterprise Workflow

Complete User & System Lifecycle Pipeline

Here is the complete sequence of how data moves through Carbonexo from user sign-up to daily tracking, twin simulation, and certified ESG impact:

1
Onboarding & Profiling
User establishes baseline habits (commute distance, diet preference, housing type, weekly reduction goal).
2
Multi-modal Ingestion
Activities logged via 1-tap quick logger, OCR utility bill scanner, or custom quantity input across 5 domains.
3
Matrix Multiplication
Core calculation engine converts activity units into kg CO₂e using verified UK DEFRA & EPA eGRID coefficients.
4
Twin Simulation
User tests counterfactual habit swaps (e.g. car to metro, beef to lentils) to forecast annual avoided CO₂e.
5
AI Grounding RAG
AI Coach accesses user metrics and peer-reviewed studies to generate actionable guidance with prompt inspection.
6
Eco Credits & Trees
Avoided emissions are minted into the double-entry credit ledger, unlocking badges and real tree certificates.
