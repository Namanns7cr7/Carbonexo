# Carbon credit

A carbon credit is a tradable instrument issued by a crediting programme (for example a voluntary carbon market registry) for a quantified greenhouse gas emission reduction or removal. One credit normally represents one metric tonne of carbon dioxide equivalent (CO2e) reduced or removed, measured against a baseline under the programme's methodology.

# How carbon credits work

A project (for example forest protection, clean cooking or direct air capture) follows a programme methodology, is validated and its results verified by an independent body, and the registry issues credits for the verified tonnes. Credits can be sold and transferred between registry accounts. When a buyer uses a credit to support a claim it is retired (cancelled) in the registry so it cannot be used again.

# Quality principles

Integrity frameworks such as the ICVCM Core Carbon Principles ask, among other things, that the reduction is additional (it would not have happened without the credit revenue), permanent or with reversal risk managed, robustly quantified and not double counted.

# Additionality

A reduction is additional when it would not have happened without the incentive from selling credits. Credits for reductions that would have happened anyway do not represent a real change.

# Retirement

Retiring a credit cancels it in the registry on behalf of a named user. Only retired credits support an offset or contribution claim; a credit that is resold has not been used.

# Allowance (cap-and-trade) versus credit

An emissions allowance is a permit under a compliance cap-and-trade system such as the EU ETS: the regulator caps total emissions and each allowance gives the holder the right to emit one tonne of CO2e. An allowance is a right to emit; a carbon credit represents a reduction or removal. They are different instruments in different markets.

# Offsets

Offsetting means retiring carbon credits to compensate for one's own emissions. An offset claim depends on credit quality and retirement; it is not the same as reducing one's own emissions.

# Carbonexo green credits are not carbon credits

Carbonexo green credits are in-app reward points earned for tracking and actions inside Carbonexo. They are not issued by a crediting programme, do not represent verified tonnes of CO2e, cannot be sold or traded and cannot be used for offset claims. They can only be redeemed for items in the Carbonexo rewards catalogue. See [Carbonexo green credits and Credit Score](/policies/green-credits-and-score.md).

# Carbon Credit Score

The Carbonexo Carbon Credit Score is an in-app engagement score, not a rating of carbon credits and not a financial credit score. See [Carbonexo green credits and Credit Score](/policies/green-credits-and-score.md).
