# Concepts

* [Emissions of one logged or hypothetical activity](activity-emissions.md) - Sanctioned computation of kg CO2(e) for a quantity of one activity, from a factor-registry record identified by factor id and dataset version.
* [Carbon Twin replace-with scenario](carbon-twin-scenario.md) - Hypothetical emissions change from replacing a recurring activity with another over a month or a year, as the Simulate screen computes it.
